How Doodle Theater works

📱 Parent's phone

Scan the QR on the TV. Snap the drawing, drag any joint dot, name the star.

no app to install

🧩 Doodle Theater server

doodle-rig (open source, MIT): cut out → joints → mesh → skin weights.

Story agent, built with Strands Agents, checks every episode with the TV's own validator.

📺 Fire TV app

Kotlin + Compose for TV. Puppets drawn live with drawVertices at 60 fps. D-pad choices, "Alexa, pause".

☁️ AWS

Amazon Bedrock · Nova 2 Lite: joints, description, stories Bedrock Guardrails: bedtime-safe Amazon Polly: neural voices + word timings
